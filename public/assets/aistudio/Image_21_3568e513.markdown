## Brand & Style

This design system targets modern B2B matchmaking, networking, and supply chain collaboration on mobile-first workflows. The design style combines modern enterprise minimalism with high-clarity transactional design: crisp white foundations, purposeful color accents, and structured metadata chips.

The tone is authoritative yet frictionless, confident, and business-focused. The user interface eliminates visual noise to prioritize business opportunities, counterparty credibility, and instant actionability. Visual hierarchy relies on typography scale and precise semantic badges rather than excessive decorative styling.

## Colors

- **Primary (`#1052E0`):** Vibrant cobalt blue dedicated to the principal calls-to-action like "Conectar", primary tab navigation states, and key interactive prompts.
- **Secondary (`#00BA63`):** Vivid emerald green reserved for active status signals (e.g., online, verified company status, active deals) and the "Ofrece" (Supply) context.
- **Tertiary (`#475569`):** Balanced slate grey for structured categorization, inactive icons, and neutral metadata chips such as secondary attributes.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`):** Deep ink navy used for crisp, high-contrast headings, text hierarchy, and structured borders.
- **Canvas & Backgrounds:** The base canvas is `#FFFFFF`, supported by a clean `#F8FAFC` for grouped card containers and `#F1F5F9` for interactive dividers and badge surfaces.
- **Demand Context:** Demand ("Demanda") badges utilize `#E06D10` (warm amber/orange) or deep slate depending on urgency, maintaining distinction from "Ofrece".

## Typography

The design system relies entirely on **Hanken Grotesk** across all roles to achieve an uncluttered, modern European tech look. 

Company titles and key business entity names must be styled with high visual prominence (`title-md` or `headline-sm`, `fontWeight: 700` or `800`). Metadata labels and status indicators leverage uppercase or sentence-cased `label-sm` with slight positive tracking to ensure instant scannability on handheld screens.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first fluid layout built on a standard 4-column system for viewports under 600px, expanding to 8 columns for tablets and 12 columns for desktop portals.

- Mobile screen margins are fixed at `1rem` (16px) to maximize horizontal data density while preserving tap ergonomics.
- Content elements and cards stack vertically with `0.75rem` to `1rem` gaps.
- Internal component padding uses `space-md` for balanced card density without crowding key information points.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is minimal, airy, and clinical. The system deliberately avoids aggressive Drop Shadows in favor of subtle layered surface contrast and ambient illumination:

- **Level 0 (Flat/Base):** Pure `#FFFFFF` background.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an ultra-soft ambient shadow: `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0px 1px 3px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` and a delicate border `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`.
- **Level 2 (Overlays & Floating Actions):** Bottom navigation bars, drawers, and modal sheets: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts **Level 2 (Rounded)** for a modern, tactile feel that softens dense corporate information without appearing childish.

- Standard cards, sheet containers, and input fields use `0.5rem` (8px).
- Action buttons, badges, and company avatar containers use `0.75rem` (12px) or full circular/pill treatment for tags.
- Tag and status chips are consistently styled as soft rounded pills (`9999px` radius) to differentiate them from square-form structural data cards.

## Components

- **Company Profile Card:** A pristine white container with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and subtle level-1 shadow. The header includes the company logo thumbnail (`rounded-md`), company name in bold (`Hanken Grotesk 700`, `#0F172A`), verified badge, and a pulsing status dot in secondary green (`#00BA63`).
- **Structured Metadata Badges ("Ofrece" & "Demanda"):**
  - **Ofrece (Offer):** Soft green background tint (`#ECFDF5`), deep green text (`#047857`), and a micro dot indicator.
  - **Demanda (Demand):** Soft blue-slate background tint (`#F1F5F9`), neutral dark text (`#334155`), with an optional orange accent line or tag for immediate contrast.
- **Primary Action ("Conecta" Button):** High-saturation vibrant blue (`#1052E0`) button with solid white bold text, height of 44px for thumb accessibility, rounded-lg (`0.75rem`), and active state feedback (scale: 0.98, background `#0C42B8`).
- **Status Indicator:** 8px circular indicator using vivid green (`#00BA63`) surrounded by a subtle 2px translucent ping/halo (`rgba(0, 186, 99, 0.2)`) denoting real-time activity and responsiveness.
- **Form Inputs:** Pure white background, `1px solid #CBD5E1`, smooth focus transition to `2px solid #1052E0` with zero glow.
- **Search & Filter Bar:** Sticky mobile search bar with embedded quick-filter chips for immediate switching between "Todo", "Ofertas", and "Demandas".
